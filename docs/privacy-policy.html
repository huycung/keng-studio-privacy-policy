QR Safe Privacy Policy

52 +

Last updated: July 9, 2026

53 + 54 +

55 + This Privacy Policy describes how QR Safe handles information when you use 56 + the QR Safe Android app. QR Safe is designed as an offline QR code and 57 + barcode scanner, generator, and review tool. 58 +

59 + 60 +

Information QR Safe accesses

61 +

62 + QR Safe requests camera access so you can scan QR codes and barcodes. The 63 + camera preview is used on your device for scanning and is not sent to a 64 + server by QR Safe. 65 +

66 +

67 + QR Safe may process QR code or barcode content that you scan or enter, 68 + including text, URLs, Wi-Fi details, contact cards, email addresses, phone 69 + numbers, or SMS-style QR content, depending on what is encoded in the QR 70 + code or barcode. 71 +

72 + 73 +

How information is used

74 +

75 + QR Safe uses scanned or entered content to show scan details, provide local 76 + URL risk warnings, generate QR codes, save scan history on your device, and 77 + support user-initiated actions such as copy, share, save QR image, open a 78 + URL, start an email, start a call intent, start an SMS intent, or add a 79 + contact through Android system apps. 80 +

81 + 82 +

Data storage and retention

83 +

84 + QR Safe stores scan history locally on your device. QR Safe does not create 85 + an account, does not require login, and does not provide cloud sync. App 86 + data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. 87 +

88 +

89 + You can delete scan history items from the History or Detail screens. If 90 + you uninstall the app, app-local data is removed by Android. QR images that 91 + you explicitly save to your gallery remain in your device gallery until you 92 + delete them. 93 +

94 + 95 +

Sharing and third parties

96 +

97 + QR Safe does not sell your personal data. QR Safe does not transmit scan 98 + history or generated QR content to a QR Safe server. 99 +

100 +

101 + When you choose a share, open, email, call, SMS, add contact, or save 102 + action, Android may send the selected content to the system app or third 103 + party app that you choose. Those apps handle the information according to 104 + their own privacy practices. 105 +

106 + 107 +

Security

108 +

109 + QR Safe keeps app data local to your device and excludes app data from 110 + Android backup. You should review scanned URLs before opening them and only 111 + share generated QR content with apps and people you trust. 112 +

113 + 114 +

Children

115 +

116 + QR Safe is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect 117 + children's personal information. 118 +

119 + 120 +

Changes to this policy

122 + This Privacy Policy may be updated when QR Safe changes how it handles 123 + information. Updates will be posted on this page. 124 +
