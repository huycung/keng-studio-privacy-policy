QR Safe Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how QR Safe handles information when you use the QR Safe Android app. QR Safe is designed as an offline QR code and barcode scanner, generator, and review tool.

Information QR Safe accesses

QR Safe requests camera access so you can scan QR codes and barcodes. The camera preview is used on your device for scanning and is not sent to a server by QR Safe.

QR Safe may process QR code or barcode content that you scan or enter, including text, URLs, Wi-Fi details, contact cards, email addresses, phone numbers, or SMS-style QR content, depending on what is encoded in the QR code or barcode.

How information is used

QR Safe uses scanned or entered content to show scan details, provide local URL risk warnings, generate QR codes, save scan history on your device, and support user-initiated actions such as copy, share, save QR image, open a URL, start an email, start a call intent, start an SMS intent, or add a contact through Android system apps.

Data storage and retention

QR Safe stores scan history locally on your device. QR Safe does not create an account, does not require login, and does not provide cloud sync. App data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer.

You can delete scan history items from the History or Detail screens. If you uninstall the app, app-local data is removed by Android. QR images that you explicitly save to your gallery remain in your device gallery until you delete them.

Sharing and third parties

QR Safe does not sell your personal data. QR Safe does not transmit scan history or generated QR content to a QR Safe server.

When you choose a share, open, email, call, SMS, add contact, or save action, Android may send the selected content to the system app or third party app that you choose. Those apps handle the information according to their own privacy practices.

Security

QR Safe keeps app data local to your device and excludes app data from Android backup. You should review scanned URLs before opening them and only share generated QR content with apps and people you trust.

Children

QR Safe is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when QR Safe changes how it handles information. Updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the QR Safe GitHub repository: https://github.com/huycung/qr-safe-privacy-policy/issues.
